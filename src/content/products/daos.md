daos is the measurement bench. A Python research library — core, research and
validation — for asking whether an agent does the same thing twice, and proving
it with numbers rather than vibes.

No UI, no terminal chrome: it's the quiet instrument the rest of the suite leans
on when a claim needs to be checked.

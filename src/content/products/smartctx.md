smartctx is a terminal-first way to build the loadout an agent starts from: which
files, which memory, which instructions. Deterministic, inspectable, and entirely
local — the context is something you assemble on purpose, not something that
happens to you.

Its palette ships from `sondalab-palette` on PyPI; its colours degrade cleanly
from truecolor to a bare `NO_COLOR` terminal.
